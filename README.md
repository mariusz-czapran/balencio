# Balencio

Balencio is a wealth, stock, and cryptocurrency portfolio simulator for Android, created for entertainment and educational purposes.

## About the app

Build a virtual fortune, manage simulated investments, and explore a modern banking-style interface without using real money.

Balencio includes:

- Custom account balance
- Simulated stock and cryptocurrency portfolios
- Bitcoin, Ethereum, and other virtual assets
- Career Mode starting with $1,000
- Progression from Level 1 to Level 10
- Global rankings with cloud synchronization
- Scheduled simulated income notifications
- Light and dark themes
- Multiple supported languages

## Career Mode

Start with a virtual balance of $1,000 and grow your simulated wealth through stocks and cryptocurrencies. Progress through ten levels and compete with other players in the global rankings.

## Premium

Balencio includes ads.

A Premium upgrade is available for **$0.99**, or the equivalent price in your local currency. Premium removes ads and unlocks additional premium features.

## Important disclaimer

Balencio is a simulator intended for entertainment and educational purposes.

It is not a bank, brokerage platform, cryptocurrency exchange, investment service, or financial institution. The app does not store, transfer, invest, generate, or trade real money, stocks, or cryptocurrencies. All balances, transactions, portfolios, profits, losses, and rankings shown in the app are simulated.
